Her story

Age is no obstacle

Judith Mariri is living proof that it is never too late to start. A former teacher, originally from Jane Furse and now at home in Bendor, Polokwane, she began baking after retiring at 50, first purely for the joy of it, and then as a business.

She funded the business with her pension and started with cakes, bread and pizza. She is accredited in Food & Beverage (Bread and Flour) through TP Dynamic Engineering Services, and works alongside two assistants. A firm believer in having more than one stream of income, she has even started designing and sewing her own leather bags.

But the teacher in her never really retired. Judith loves sharing her skills so that others can start businesses of their own, and she has already trained students, which is why she now offers baking lessons.
